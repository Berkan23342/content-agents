# Outbreak Diary: Floor 41, story bible

The one place that remembers this story. The writer reads it before every part and updates **Current state** and
**Episode log** after writing one. Everything here is original and fictional.

## The world

- **Port Ellis**: the same rainy harbour city as the other Outbreak Diary stories, on the same night. Never name real
  cities, brands or people.
- **Meridian Tower**, a 52-storey glass office tower in the financial district.
  - **Floor 41:** the drawing studio of a small architecture firm, with desks, a meeting room with frosted glass and a
    break room.
  - **Floor 40:** the cafeteria.
  - **Floor 30:** a glass skybridge to the neighbouring Calder Tower.
  - **Roof (52):** a helipad.
- **Power:** a backup generator keeps the emergency lights on for about a week. The main elevators stop on Day 2; the
  service elevator may still work.
- **Harbor Station** is on the radio, saying boats leave Pier Four at dawn.

## The infected ("them")

Same rules as every Outbreak Diary story:

- They hunt by sound.
- They are clumsy on stairs and cannot open doors with handles.
- They are slow by day and restless at night.
- A bite brings fever, then silence, then change.
- In the tower they crowd the lobby and the stairwells. Anything that makes noise brings them up.

## People

- **Maya (the narrator)**, twenty-six, a junior architect. Precise and anxious; she counts things to stay calm. She
  built a scale model of the whole city for work, so she knows every street and roof.
- **Dev**, the night security guard, mid-fifties and a former firefighter. Calm, hums old songs, has a bad knee and
  the master keycards.
- **Ruth**, the receptionist on floor 12. Only a voice on the intercom so far.
- **A light in Calder Tower:** someone across the gap flashes a torch at night.

## Season 1 plan (about 30 parts; adapt freely)

1. **Parts 1-5. The night it started.** The elevator, Dev, the lockdown, the first look down.
2. **Parts 6-12. Holding floor 41.** The cafeteria run to floor 40. Ruth's voice from floor 12. Torch signals from
   Calder Tower.
3. **Parts 13-20. Losing power.** The main elevators die. The skybridge on floor 30. Dev's knee. A storm.
4. **Parts 21-30. The roof.** A helicopter rumour, the climb to 52, and a choice that opens season 2.

## Voices

- The narrator is always the woman telling the story.
- A character can say a line in their own voice: put the spoken words alone in a beat with `"speaker"`. For example,
  `{"text": "Stay away from the door.", "speaker": "..."}`. The narration around it says who speaks ("Then a voice
  whispered.").
- Speakers in this story: `dev`, `ruth`, `announcer` (the Harbor Station radio voice). Anyone else is quoted by the narrator.
- Use one or two spoken lines per part. They make the story feel alive.

## Rules for every part

- **One part is one day.** Part N is Day N: the video shows "DAY N" by itself, and a first line that names the day says
  "Day N." Never give two parts the same day.
- 45-65 seconds: 100-125 words, 8-14 beats.
- The first line is a hook. The last line is a cliffhanger.
- First person, past tense, short sentences.
- PG-13: tension and danger, never gore, no swearing.
- Every part moves the story forward a little.
- **Scenes to use:** `tower`, `office`, `elevator`, `window` (the street far below), `hallway`, `stairwell`, `rooftop`,
  `kitchen` (the break room), `phone`, `radio` (Dev's radio), `supplies`, `door`. Never use `building`, `room` or
  `peephole` (those are Sam's apartment).

## Episode log

- **Part 1 (Day 1) Working Late:** Maya is working late. Glass breaks below and the alert arrives. The elevator comes
  up with something inside it. She hides, and a flashlight finds the floor.
- **Part 2 (Day 2) Security:** Dev is real. He locks the floor, the elevators die, and Ruth calls on the radio from floor 12.
- **Part 3 (Day 3) Ruth on Twelve:** Maya and Dev call the service elevator for Ruth. Ruth screams and goes silent, and
  the elevator climbs toward 41.
- **Part 4 (Day 4) The Number Is Climbing:** the elevator stops on 41. It is empty except for Ruth's ringing phone, which stops when someone answers. A torch flashes from Calder Tower.
