# Outbreak Diary: The Last Light, story bible

The one place that remembers this story. The writer reads it before every part and updates **Current state** and
**Episode log** after writing one. Everything here is original and fictional.

## The world

- **Port Ellis** and its coast, the same rainy region and outbreak as the other Outbreak Diary stories. Never name real
  places, brands or people.
- **Skerry Head**, a rock beside Port Ellis, joined to the shore by one narrow causeway. On it: a tall lighthouse (two
  hundred steps), an oil store, a generator shed, a boathouse and dunes behind.
- **The light:** the great lamp guides ships, but the infected follow light too. A beam draws them down the causeway.
- **Harbor Station** is on the radio, saying boats leave Pier Four at dawn.

## The infected ("them")

Same rules as every Outbreak Diary story:

- They hunt by sound, and here also by light.
- They are clumsy on stairs and cannot open doors with handles.
- They are slow by day and restless at night.
- A bite brings fever, then silence, then change.
- **Here:** a bright beam pulls them to the causeway; darkness loses them, and it loses ships too.

## People

- **Ilsa (the narrator)**, forty, the lighthouse keeper. Steady, dry, counts steps and keeps a log.
- **Pip**, eleven, hiding in the oil store. Quiet, knows the old machinery.
- **Oren**, a man's voice on the radio, captain of a boat with forty survivors steering for the light.

## Season plan (ends on Day 10)

The story ends on Day 10 (story/series.json `finale`); after that its slot goes to a new story.

- **Days 1-3.** Keep the light alive, the boat, Pip, and the three flashes from the dunes.
- **Days 4-7.** Who signals from the dunes, a storm, fuel, a way to lead the dead off the causeway with a second light.
- **Days 8-9.** The boat in danger, the plan to guide it past the reef.
- **Day 10, the finale.** Ilsa lights one last beam, the boat sails to Pier Four, and she ends on one image (the lamp
  turning slowly in the dawn). No cliffhanger that needs a Day 11.

## Voices

- The narrator is always the woman telling the story.
- Speakers: `pip` (a boy), `oren` (a man), `announcer` (the Harbor Station radio voice). Anyone else is quoted by the narrator.
- Use one or two spoken lines per part.

## Rules for every part

- **One part is one day.** Part N is Day N; never open with "Day N."
- 100-125 words, 8-14 beats; the first line a hook, the last a cliffhanger.
- First person, past tense, short sentences. A diary: Ilsa's lighthouse log.
- PG-13: tension and danger, never gore, no swearing, animals never die.
- **Scenes to use:** `rooftop` (the lantern room), `stairwell`, `window`, `door`, `radio`, `phone`, `supplies`,
  `kitchen`, `lake` (the sea, with `canoe`), `forest` (the dunes), `truck`, `gas_station`, `lobby`. Never use
  `building`, `room` or `peephole`.
