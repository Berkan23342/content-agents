# Outbreak Diary: Pine Hollow, story bible

The one place that remembers this story. The writer reads it before every part and updates **Current state** and
**Episode log** after writing one. Everything here is original and fictional.

## The world

- **Pine Hollow Lake**, an hour north of Port Ellis in pine-covered hills. Never name real places, brands or people.
- **The ranger cabin** on the shore. It has a stone fireplace, a ranger radio base station, a dock with a canoe, and an
  old pickup with half a tank.
- **Cedar Falls**, a small town eight miles down the road. It went quiet on Day 1.
- **Harbor Station** is on the radio, saying boats leave Pier Four in Port Ellis at dawn.

## The infected ("them")

Same rules as every Outbreak Diary story:

- They hunt by sound.
- They are clumsy and cannot open doors with handles.
- They are slow by day and restless at night.
- A bite brings fever, then silence, then change.
- **Here:** they cannot swim. They sink and keep walking along the lake bottom, so the water is safe until it isn't.
  Cold slows them down.

## People

- **Nora (the narrator)**, thirty-eight, a park ranger. Quiet and practical, with a dry sense of humour. She reads
  tracks and birds. She promised her sister she would keep her daughter safe.
- **Ava**, sixteen, her niece, at the cabin for autumn break. Sarcastic and brave, and she will not stop trying to
  reach her mom.
- **Grace**, Ava's mother and Nora's sister, a nurse in Port Ellis. Her last message: "Stay with Nora. Don't come to
  the city."
- **Tess**, a ranger at the north fire tower fifteen miles away. A voice on the radio.

## Season 1 plan (about 30 parts; adapt freely)

1. **Parts 1-5. The first nights.** The radio, the ranger at the window, the canoe, the first lesson about water.
2. **Parts 6-12. Cedar Falls.** A supply run in the pickup. Lost hikers. Tess on the radio.
3. **Parts 13-20. The island.** A refuge in the middle of the lake. Winter coming. Someone who isn't who they say.
4. **Parts 21-30. The city.** The drive towards Port Ellis to find Grace, and an ending that opens season 2.

## Voices

- The narrator is always the woman telling the story.
- A character can say a line in their own voice: put the spoken words alone in a beat with `"speaker"`. For example,
  `{"text": "Stay away from the door.", "speaker": "..."}`. The narration around it says who speaks ("Then a voice
  whispered.").
- Speakers in this story: `ava`, `tess` (on the ranger radio), `grace` (Ava's mom, if she is ever heard), `announcer` (the Harbor Station radio voice). Anyone else is quoted by the narrator.
- Use one or two spoken lines per part. They make the story feel alive.

## Rules for every part

- **One part is one day.** Part N is Day N: the video shows "DAY N" by itself, and a first line that names the day says
  "Day N." Never give two parts the same day.
- 45-65 seconds: 100-125 words, 8-14 beats.
- The first line is a hook. The last line is a cliffhanger.
- First person, past tense, short sentences.
- PG-13: tension and danger, never gore, no swearing.
- Every part moves the story forward a little.
- **Scenes to use:** `forest`, `cabin`, `truck`, `radio` (the ranger radio), `phone`, `supplies`, `door`, `street`
  (Cedar Falls), `rooftop` (the fire tower). Never use `building`, `room`, `peephole` or `window` (those are city
  apartments).
- **New places to visit often:** `lake`, `gas_station`, `supermarket` and `school` (Cedar Falls), `hospital` (the clinic).

## Episode log

- **Part 1 (Day 1) The Quiet Town:** the birds go silent. The radio fills with Cedar Falls calling for help, then
  nothing. Grace's message gets through. At midnight something walks out of the trees and stops at the cabin window,
  wearing a ranger's uniform.
- **Part 2 (Day 2) The Ranger at the Glass:** the infected ranger is Walt, the trail keeper. Tess says to go to the water.
  Ava lures Walt away with a thrown can, and the canoe is gone from the dock.
- **Part 3 (Day 3) Who Took the Canoe:** small boot prints show a child or a scared person took it. At dusk it drifts
  with a red scarf, and a dozen of them follow its trail to the cabin.
- **Part 4 (Day 4) The Girl With the Scarf:** Pip lands at the dock with the dozen behind her. Nora lures them into the lake with a banged pot. Pip says her brother is still on the island.
- **Part 5 (Day 5) The Island:** Pip explains about Jonah and the lantern. A light blinks on the island, then goes dark, and a second light appears much closer.
- **Part 6 (Day 6) The Lantern in the Reeds:** Nora finds a warm lantern and an empty rowboat. It is not Jonah's. Boot prints lead to the porch and a board creaks.
- **Part 7 (Day 7) Someone on the Porch:** a note offers Jonah in return for rowing out alone at dusk. Nora and Ava paddle out and meet a man in Walt's ranger jacket.
